Why it gets dishes right

Your house glossary

Correct it once

Hyur keeps a glossary of how your dishes are named and explained. The model has to follow it, and when you fix a dish, your wording wins on every future menu.

  • Click Correct on any dish to save your wording
  • Prices and dish order never pass through the model
  • Meat is never tagged “vegetarian”; pork is checked in code
